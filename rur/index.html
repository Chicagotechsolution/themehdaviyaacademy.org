Mehdaviya Academy mein khush aamdeed

Yeh internet par ek chhota sa madrasa hai, janne ke shauqeen bachon ke liye. Yahan aap Mahdavia riwayat ke baare mein seekhenge — ek Musalman biradari jiski kahani paanch sau saal se bhi pehle Hindustan mein shuru hui.

Aap ek dana ustaad se milenge, ek bahaduri ki kahani sunenge, dekhenge ke gharane kaise namaz parhte aur baant-te hain, aur un sawalon ke jawab payenge jo bachay sach much poochte hain.

Mahdavi Musalman hain. Woh din mein paanch namazein parhte hain, Ramzan ke rozay rakhte hain, ghareebon ko dete hain aur Hajj karte hain — bilkul duniya bhar ke Musalmanon ki tarah.

Bachon ke sawalon ki taraf jayen

Hamari kahani: Jaunpur ke ustaad

Mahdaviyon ka iman hai ke bahut pehle Allah ne ek khaas rehnuma bheja jise Mahdi kehte hain, aur woh Jaunpur shehar ke ek meherban aur dana ustaad Syed Muhammad thay. Mahdavi unhe pyar se Mehdi Mau'ood kehte hain. Yeh unki kahani hai, jaise biradari sunati hai.

  1. 1443

    Jaunpur ka ek bacha

    San 1443 mein Hindustan ke shehar Jaunpur mein Syed Muhammad naam ka ek bacha paida hua. Unhe Qur'an se mohabbat thi aur baray ho kar woh ek mashhoor ustaad banay.

  2. 1496

    Makkah mein elaan

    Syed Muhammad door ka safar kar ke muqaddas shehar Makkah pahunche. Wahan Ka'aba ke samne unhon ne elaan kiya ke Allah ne unhe Mahdi — wada kiya hua rehnuma — chuna hai. Mahdavi is par pooray dil se iman rakhte hain.

  3. ~1499

    Ahmedabad mein taleem

    Wapas Hindustan mein, Ahmedabad shehar mein, unhon ne phir yahi elaan kiya aur bahut se logon ko taleem di. Unhon ne kaha ke Allah se paison se zyada mohabbat karo aur har roz Allah ko yaad karo. Bahut se gharanon ne unki pairavi chuni.

  4. 1505

    Farah mein alvida

    Woh san 1505 mein Farah nami jagah par, jo aaj ke Afghanistan mein hai, wafat pa gaye. Unke shagird bhi ustaad banay aur unke sabaq naslon tak aaj bhi pahunchte rahe.

Bahadur Syed Khundmir

Mehdi Mau'ood ki wafat ke baad unke mannay walon ko naye rehnuma chahiye thay. Sab se pyare rehnumaon mein Hazrat Bandagi Miyan Syed Khundmir thay. Woh un logon ki rehnumai karte thay jo apne din Allah ki yaad mein guzarte thay.

San 1523 mein ek taqatwar sultan ka darbar Mahdaviyon ke aqeeday par naraz hua. Syed Khundmir aur unke saathi pur-aman thay aur unke paas hathyar nahin thay, magar unhon ne apna iman nahin chhora. Woh aur unke bahut se saathi apne iman ki khatir shaheed kar diye gaye.

Mahdavi unhe shaheed aur himmat-o-iman ka hero mante hain. Hindustan mein Champaner mein unki aaram gah par door door se gharane mohabbat se hazri dete hain.

Mahdavi kaise rehte hain

Mahdavi gharane duniya bhar ke Musalman gharanon ki tarah rehte hain, aur unki apni kuch khoobsurat riwayatein bhi hain.

  • Paanch arkaan

    Mahdavi Islam ke paanchon arkaan par amal karte hain: shahadat kehna, din mein paanch namazein, Ramzan ke rozay, zakat dena, aur Hajj karna.

  • Subah aur sehpahar ka zikr

    Fajr ki namaz aur Asr ki namaz ke baad Mahdavi khamoshi se baith kar Allah ke naam dohrate hain. Allah ki is yaad ko zikr kehte hain.

  • Daira ki zindagi

    Daira ka matlab hai 'halqa'. Yeh Mahdaviyon ka mohalla ya community center hai jahan gharane mil kar namaz parhte, seekhte aur khushiyan manate hain.

  • Daswan hissa baantna

    Mahdavi ushr par amal karte hain: jo kamayen ya ugayen uska daswan hissa doosron ki madad ke liye dete hain taake biradari mein koi peechhe na reh jaye.

  • Allah par bharosa

    Tawakkul ka matlab hai Allah par bharosa karna, jaise parinda bharosa karta hai ke usay har subah dana milega. Mahdavi dil ko pursukoon aur bharose wala rakhne ki koshish karte hain.

Bachon ke sawal

Jawab dekhne ke liye sawal par dabayen.

Mehdi Mau'ood kaun thay?

Mehdi Mau'ood ka matlab hai 'wada kiye hue Mahdi'. Yeh woh pyara laqab hai jo Mahdavi Jaunpur ke Syed Muhammad ko dete hain. Mahdaviyon ka iman hai ke Allah ne unhe logon ko neki ki taraf wapas lane ke liye bheja.

Kya Mahdavi Musalman hain?

Ji haan, bilkul! Mahdavi Musalman hain. Woh shahadat kehte hain, paanch namazein parhte hain, Ramzan ke rozay rakhte hain, sadqa dete hain aur Hajj karte hain. Unka khaas aqeeda yeh hai ke wada kiye hue Mahdi aa chuke hain.

Daira kya hai?

Daira ka matlab hai 'halqa'. Yeh Mahdaviyon ka mohalla ya community center hai. Wahan gharane mil kar namaz parhte, seekhte aur ek baray khandaan ki tarah ek doosre ki madad karte hain.

Zikr mein kya hota hai?

Log khamoshi se baithte hain, aksar Fajr aur Asr ki namazon ke baad, aur dheere dheere Allah ke naam aur tareefein dohrate hain. Is se dil ko sukoon milta hai, jaise Allah ke saath ek gehri pursukoon saans lena.

Kya Mahdavi din mein paanch namazein parhte hain?

Ji haan. Mahdavi wohi paanch namazein parhte hain jo duniya bhar ke Musalman parhte hain. Unke roohani rehnuma, jinhe murshid kehte hain, namaz parhate hain.

Kya Mahdavi Ramzan ke rozay rakhte hain?

Ji haan. Mahdavi gharane doosre Musalmanon ki tarah Ramzan ke rozay rakhte hain aur namaz, khanay aur khushi ke saath Eid manate hain.

Syed Khundmir kaun thay?

Hazrat Bandagi Miyan Syed Khundmir ibtidai Mahdaviyon ke bahadur rehnuma thay. San 1523 mein pur-aman hone ke bawajood unhe unke iman ki wajah se shaheed kar diya gaya. Mahdavi unhe shaheed aur hero ke taur par yaad karte hain.

Ushr kya hai?

Ushr daswan hissa dena hai. Agar aap ne das sikkay kamaye to ek baant denge. Mahdavi apni kamai ya fasl ka daswan hissa baant-te hain taake biradari mein sab ka khayal rakha jaye.

Tawakkul ka kya matlab hai?

Tawakkul ka matlab hai Allah par bharosa. Aap mehnat bhi karte hain aur apni poori koshish karte hain — phir dil ko aaram dete hain, is bharose ke saath ke Allah aap ka khayal rakh raha hai.

Aaj Mahdavi kahan rehte hain?

Bahut se Hindustan mein rehte hain, khaas taur par Hyderabad mein, aur Gujarat, Karnataka aur doosri jagahon par bhi. Kuch gharane Pakistan mein hain, aur kuch door America ke shehar Chicago jaise sheharon mein rehte hain.

Nanhi lughat

Chhote lafz, baray maani. Inhe buland aawaz mein bolen!

Zikr
Allah ki yaad: khamoshi se uske naam dohrana, khaas taur par Fajr aur Asr ki namazon ke baad.
Bai'at
Wafadari ka wada: ustaad ke haath mein haath rakh kar Allah ke rastay par chalne ka wada karna.
Murshid
Roohani ustaad jo namaz parhata hai aur logon ko Allah ke qareeb lane mein madad karta hai.
Tark-e-dunya
'Duniyavi cheezein chhorna': paison ki kam aur Allah ki zyada fikr karna. Iska matlab kabhi apna khandaan chhorna nahin.
Hijrat
Iman ki khatir nayi jagah ja basna.
Tawakkul
Allah par bharosa ke woh aap ka khayal rakhega.
Ushr
Kamai ya fasl ka daswan hissa doosron ki madad ke liye dena.
Khalifa
Woh rehnuma jo bani ke baad biradari ki rehnumai kare.
Shaheed
Jo apne iman ke liye jaan de, jaise Hazrat Syed Khundmir.

Sachi biradariyon se milen

Mahdavia biradariyan aaj bhi sach much aabad hain. America mein Mahdavia Islamic Center of Chicago rozana aur Jumma ki namazein karata hai, Qur'an aur Arabi sikhata hai aur zaroratmand gharanon ki madad karta hai.

Hindustan mein Hyderabad ki Anjuman-e-Mahdavia san 1902 se biradari ka bara markaz hai. Agar aap kabhi kisi Mahdavi biradari mein jayen to aap ka garmjoshi se istiqbal hoga.
